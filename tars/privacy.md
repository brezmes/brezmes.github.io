# TARS — Privacy policy

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

TARS is a private, single-user assistant operated by Alberto Brezmes for his own use. It is not offered to the public.

**Data accessed.** With the owner's explicit OAuth consent, TARS accesses the owner's own Gmail messages and Google Calendar events.

**Use.** The data is used only to provide the owner with summaries, reminders and actions he requests. Sending email or changing events requires his confirmation.

**Storage.** OAuth tokens and any cached data are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Content may be processed by the AI model provider the owner has chosen, solely to answer his requests.

**Google API Services.** TARS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

**Revocation.** Access can be revoked at any time at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.
